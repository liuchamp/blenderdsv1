| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Stage 0 | `G G` / `Alt+LMB` / `Ctrl+Alt+LMB` | 边滑动 / 选环 / 选圈 | ⬜ |
| Stage 0 | `Ctrl+E`（边菜单）→ Mark Sharp / Mark Seam | 标记硬边与缝合边 | ⬜ |
